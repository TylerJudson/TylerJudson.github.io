CourseSync

AI-Powered Course Management for Students

In Development Desktop App

Intelligent Course Management

CourseSync is an educational productivity application designed specifically for students. It seamlessly syncs assignments from Canvas LMS and BYU Learning Suite, providing a unified view of all your coursework with AI-powered assistance to help you succeed.

Built with Flutter for native desktop performance and powered by RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) technology, CourseSync combines comprehensive course management with an intelligent AI tutor that understands your specific course materials and can answer questions instantly.

Core Features

Assignment Sync

Automatically fetch and sync assignments from Canvas LMS and BYU Learning Suite. Stay on top of all your deadlines with a unified view of coursework across multiple platforms.

AI Chat Tutor

Ask questions about your course materials and get instant, context-aware answers. The AI tutor uses RAG technology to retrieve relevant information from your course documents and provide accurate, helpful responses.

Document Management

Store and organize all your course documents in one place. Documents are automatically indexed and made searchable, enabling the AI to provide accurate answers based on your actual course content.

Multi-Platform Support

Native desktop application for macOS, Windows, and Linux. Enjoy a consistent, high-performance experience on whichever platform you prefer.

Bring Your Own Key (BYOK)

Use your own API keys from OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google for AI features. You have full control over your AI provider and usage costs.

Privacy First

Your course data stays local on your device. No server required - all data processing happens on your machine with optional cloud AI for the chat features.

Built with Modern Technologies

CourseSync leverages cutting-edge technologies to deliver a powerful, efficient desktop application with state-of-the-art AI capabilities.

Flutter Desktop ObjectBox Database RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) Vector Search (HNSW) Riverpod State Management Node.js Scraper Playwright

The application uses ObjectBox for local-first data storage with HNSW vector indexing for lightning-fast semantic search. Course materials are automatically embedded and indexed, enabling the AI to quickly retrieve relevant context for answering your questions.

Technical Excellence

RAG-Powered AI

Advanced Retrieval-Augmented Generation system that combines vector search with large language models to provide accurate, context-aware answers based on your actual course materials.

Local-First Architecture

All your data stays on your device using ObjectBox for fast, reliable local storage. No server dependencies mean you can work offline and maintain complete privacy.

Intelligent Scraping

Node.js subprocess with Playwright for reliable, efficient scraping of Canvas and Learning Suite. Handles authentication flows and extracts course data seamlessly.

Multi-Provider AI

Support for OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, and Google Gemini. Choose the AI provider that best fits your needs and budget, or bring your own API key.
