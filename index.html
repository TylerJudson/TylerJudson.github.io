About Me

I study computer science at Brigham Young University (GPA 4.0) with a minor in entrepreneurship, and I graduate in April 2028. At Amplify I am leading the re-architecture of our automatic dubbing pipeline for video and live streams. I also co-founded LOOP, paper math practice for grades K–6, where I am the only developer.

I mostly work agentic-first now: I write the spec and the tests before any code, then I direct AI agents through the build. I own the design and I own checking that it actually works.
